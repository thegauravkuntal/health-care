About DAVA24

Care Never Sleeps

At DAVA24, we believe healthcare should always remain accessible, affordable, and dependable because “Care Never Sleeps.” DAVA24 is a growing offline medical store franchise chain and 24-hours operating medical shop network committed to providing genuine medicines, healthcare products, wellness essentials, and personal care solutions at affordable prices across India.

We provide genuine prescription medicines, over-the-counter medicines, healthcare products, wellness supplements, and personal care essentials through our growing PAN India pharmacy network.

At DAVA24, we understand that healthcare needs can arise at any time. That is why our stores operate with a commitment to serving communities with reliable healthcare solutions, quality medicines, and professional support whenever needed.

Through our expanding franchise network and customer-focused approach, we ensure quick medicine availability and trusted healthcare services closer to people's homes.

We aim to simplify healthcare by delivering authentic products at competitive prices while maintaining the highest standards of trust, quality, and customer satisfaction.

Whether you need daily healthcare essentials, wellness products, medical support, or prescribed medicines, DAVA24 stands committed to providing affordable healthcare solutions with convenience, care, and reliability.

DAVA24 is more than a pharmacy chain — it is a healthcare partner built on trust, affordability, quality, and commitment to customer well-being.
